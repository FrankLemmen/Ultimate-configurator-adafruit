Step 1

Connect your MacroPad

Your configs live on the MacroPad itself. Choose its drive so this page can read and save them.

  1. Plug in the MacroPad. It appears as a drive called CIRCUITPY.
  2. Click Select drive. Choose CIRCUITPY (for example E:\ on Windows, or CIRCUITPY under Locations on a Mac).
  3. Allow the page to view and edit files when the browser asks.
This browser can't open drives. Open this page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on Windows or macOS. Safari and Firefox don't support saving to a drive from a web page.

Your configs

Turn the knob on the MacroPad to switch between them, in this order.

Turn the knobLoads the next or previous config. Its name appears on the display at once.
Push the knobSleep: all lights and the display go off and the keys do nothing. Push again to wake up.
DimmingAfter the dim time the lights go off, but the keys keep working. Any key press turns them back on.

● Unsaved changes
Click a key to edit it. The screen shows exactly what the MacroPad will show.
